The demo will show an initial implementation, identify a spacing or background issue, ask the agent to revisit the design data and update the code, and compare the result at the same viewport.

## What each example will document

Published examples will include a design-to-implementation comparison, the client and model, runtime and design plugin versions, viewport, and font conditions. Initial and refined results will be shown separately, with manual interventions and remaining differences recorded.

This page does not yet contain recorded examples or standardized benchmark conclusions. Share your experience in [Discussions](https://github.com/tarot-ai-labs/tarot-pixel/discussions).
